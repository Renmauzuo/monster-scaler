Summon a spirit

Generates a statblock for the Tasha's Cauldron of Everything summon spells. Enter the spell slot level you're casting at and your spell attack modifier and spell save DC, and the spirit's stats will be filled in accordingly.

Direct link




Using this tool

The summon spells from Tasha's Cauldron of Everything use a single stat block whose armor class, hit points, number of attacks, and damage scale with the level of the spell slot you use, while the attack rolls and save DCs come from your own spell attack modifier and spell save DC. Pick the spell, choose the form (the subtype you select when casting), then enter the slot level and your caster stats. The generated stat block updates live and can be exported or added to the encounter tracker.
